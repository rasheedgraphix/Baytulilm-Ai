Baytul Ilm ("we", "our", or "us") is an Islamic educational and spiritual mobile application developed by Hafiz Nouman Ur Rasheed. The platform provides curriculum textbooks for Dars-e-Nizami, Quran Pak with tafseer, authentic hadith collections, daily supplications, digital Tasbeeh counter, Qibla compass, prayer times, and live official broadcasts from Makkah and Madinah (Haramain Live).

We hold user privacy in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy discloses our exact practices regarding data collection, permissions usage, storage, and user control, in full adherence to the Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google Play Families Policy, and the Amazon Appstore Content Guidelines.

🔒 Our Core Privacy Principles

  • Zero Commercial Ads: Baytul Ilm does NOT serve commercial third-party banner, interstitial, or tracking ads.
  • We NEVER Sell or Monetize Your Data: We will never sell, lease, trade, or share your personal information with data brokers or marketing agencies.
  • Guest Mode by Default: You do not need to register or create an account to read books, access Quranic tafseer, or use prayer times.

1. Permissions Disclosed (Mandatory for Google Play & Amazon Appstore)

To deliver core Islamic services, our application requests the following Android permissions. Each permission is strictly bounded to its designated functionality:

android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

Purpose: To calculate exact local prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), sunrise/sunset times, and determine the accurate azimuth heading for the Qibla Compass pointing to the Holy Kaaba in Makkah based on the user's geographic coordinates.

Handling: Location data is processed strictly on-device in real-time. The app DOES NOT track location in the background when the app is closed, does not maintain a history of your whereabouts, and does not upload your coordinates to external ad networks or tracking servers.

android.permission.CAMERA

Purpose: Requested exclusively when the user accesses the optional "Book Page OCR / AI Scanner" feature to capture an image of an Arabic or Urdu book page for text recognition and study transcription.

Handling: Captured images are processed solely for optical character extraction. Photos are NEVER stored permanently on remote servers and are NEVER used for facial or biometric recognition. The camera is marked with android:required="false" so devices without a camera can still run the app.

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Purpose: Used to stream authorized public live feeds from Masjid al-Haram (Makkah) and Masjid an-Nabawi (Madinah), download Islamic books and PDF files for offline reading, and receive cloud database updates.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Purpose: Used optionally to trigger timely prayer alerts (Adhan reminders), daily Hadith notifications, and curriculum announcements at scheduled times even when the device is resting.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Purpose: Provides subtle tactile haptic vibration when incrementing the digital Tasbeeh counter to allow eyes-free recitation.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to register an account, your name and email are securely handled via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Local Persistence (Android Room SQLite): Your bookmarks, reading history, personal notes, quiz scores, and Tasbeeh counters are stored in an encrypted local database on your physical device.
  • App Preferences: Language preferences (Urdu, English, Pashto, Arabic) and visual theme settings are preserved locally on-device.
  • Crash & Performance Diagnostics: Anonymous technical logs, device model, OS version, and crash stack traces (via Google Firebase Crashlytics) to diagnose bugs and improve app reliability.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with industry-leading, privacy-certified infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase & Google Play Services: For authentication, cloud messaging (FCM), and crash diagnostic reports.
  • Official Haramain Broadcasting: Official live streaming feeds directly from the official Haramain platforms and verified YouTube embeds.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Baytul Ilm is an educational, wholesome Islamic application suitable for users of all ages, including children and madrasa students. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • The application contains zero inappropriate, offensive, or age-restricted content.
  • No behavioral profiling or interest-based advertising is performed.

5. Mandatory User Rights & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's mandatory Data Deletion Policy, users retain total control over their personal information:

  • In-App Deletion: You can wipe all locally stored reading records, bookmarks, and notes anytime via the Profile/Settings screen or by clearing the app cache in Android Settings.
  • Cloud Account Deletion: To request complete and permanent deletion of your account and cloud records, email us at hafiznoumanurrasheed4@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account / Data". All associated data will be permanently wiped from our systems within 48 hours.
