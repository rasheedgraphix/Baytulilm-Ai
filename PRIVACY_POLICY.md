# Privacy Policy for Baytul Ilm AI

**Last updated:** September 11, 2026

**Baytul Ilm AI** ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing an authentic and comprehensive Islamic educational platform through curriculum books, Dars-e-Nizami resources, Quranic tafseer, Hadith collections, live Haramain broadcasts, and Islamic AI learning tools. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, store, and protect your information when using our Android mobile application ("Baytul Ilm AI" or the "App").

By installing or using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

#### A. Information You Directly Provide
* **Account Registration:** When creating an account or logging in via Firebase Authentication, we collect your name, email address, and unique user ID.
* **Study & Academic Data:** Bookmarks, personal reading notes, quiz scores, reading progress, and digital Tasbeeh counter history.
* **AI Inquiries:** Questions and prompts submitted to the Islamic AI Scholar / AI Assistant tools to retrieve educational information.

#### B. Device Permissions & Automatically Collected Data
* **Location (Coarse/Fine):** Used **strictly on-device** to calculate local Islamic prayer times (Salah times) and point the Qibla Compass accurately. Location data is never stored on external servers or shared with advertisers.
* **Storage & Downloaded Files:** Used to download and save Islamic books (PDFs) locally on your device for offline reading and study.
* **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** Used optionally to receive daily prayer reminders, new book publications, and essential curriculum announcements.
* **Audio Playback:** Used solely for playing authentic Islamic audio (Quran recitation, Darood-o-Salam, and Adhan alerts). The app does not record audio or access your microphone.
* **Diagnostics & App Performance:** Anonymous technical logs, app version, device model, and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) to diagnose issues and optimize app performance.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate educational purposes:
1. To provide uninterrupted access to Islamic curriculum books, PDFs, and offline reading.
2. To provide Islamic AI educational assistance (utilizing multi-engine AI models).
3. To calculate accurate prayer schedules and Qibla direction based on geographic coordinates.
4. To deliver important announcements, updates, and prayer notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
5. To maintain user study progress, bookmarked pages, and quiz records.
6. To detect and fix technical bugs, crashes, and performance issues.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

We integrate only with reputable third-party services that maintain strict security and confidentiality standards:
* **Google Firebase & Google Play Services:** For secure authentication, cloud synchronization, crash analytics, and push notifications.
* **Generative AI Engines (Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Anthropic, OpenAI):** For processing educational academic queries without linking to personal identities.
* **Haramain Official Broadcasting:** Streamed via official, verified CDN / YouTube live endpoints from Masjid al-Haram and Masjid an-Nabawi.

We **NEVER** sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information with advertising brokers or commercial data vendors.

---

### 4. Data Security and Retention

* **Data Security:** We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS, TLS, and secure Firebase security rules) to safeguard all data during transmission and storage.
* **Data Retention:** Your account data and educational progress are retained only as long as you maintain an active account with us.

---

### 5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data:
* **Access and Correction:** You can view and update your profile details anytime in the Profile and Settings screens.
* **Account and Data Deletion:** You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud data directly within the app under **Settings > Delete Account**, or by emailing us at `hafiznoumanurrasheed4@gmail.com`. Upon request, all your account records, bookmarks, and quiz histories will be permanently purged within 30 days.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Policy Compliance)

Baytul Ilm AI is an educational Islamic application suitable for students and users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verified parental consent. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal details without consent, we will immediately delete that information from our servers.
